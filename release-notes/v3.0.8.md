## New: Reset graphics driver

Screen flickering, black or blank windows, or a frozen display? **Help and troubleshooting > Reset graphics driver** restarts the display driver without a reboot. It does the same thing as Windows' little-known Win+Ctrl+Shift+B shortcut.

- Your screens go black and beep for about two seconds, then come back. Open programs keep running.
- It asks before doing anything, and warns you if a game is running (fullscreen games may crash or minimize).
- Works on every laptop and handheld, with or without G-Helper.
- If the GPU has stopped responding completely, Windows handles that itself and this button can't help.

**Install:** download `PerformanceCompanion-3.0.8.ahk` below and double-click it, or use **Update** in the tray menu. Settings are kept.
